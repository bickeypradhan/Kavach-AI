PS6: Build a prototype AI system that detects: Safety gear compliance
Factories face constant risk from unsafe worker practices and hazardous incidents
such as smoke and fire. Yet detection and response are often delayed or
inconsistent. Many industrial sites rely on manual monitoring, periodic
inspections, or standalone alarms that may not quickly identify unsafe gear usage
or provide timely, location-specific evidence of smoke or fire. This leads to slower
evacuation, increased damage, and higher risk of injury or worse outcomes.
There is a need for an AI-based, real-time monitoring system that can automatically
detect whether workers are wearing required safety gear and identify early signs of
smoke and fire within factory premises. The solution should operate reliably in
dynamic industrial environments, support fast alerting to the right personnel, and
help reduce incident response time by providing actionable insights such as the
